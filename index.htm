About UZ Industry

Built in Sialkot. Led by Umer.

UZ Industry is a custom sportswear manufacturer from Sialkot, Pakistan, focused on premium teamwear for clubs, schools, brands and organizations worldwide. Founded by Umer, the company is committed to quality, customization and reliable production for every order.
